- Confucius

Success

"Success is not final, failure is not fatal: It is the courage to continue that counts."
- Winston S. Churchill

Dreams

"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."
- Eleanor Roosevelt

Quotes inspire people because they capture wisdom, motivation, and experiences in a few powerful words. They encourage reflection, spark creativity, and remind us of our potential to overcome challenges and achieve our goals.
